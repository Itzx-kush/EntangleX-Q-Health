# Phase 18 — Evidence Cockpit and Product Experience

Phase 18 adds the product layer needed to make the research platform usable without changing the certified scientific workflow.

## Delivered

- Animated EntangleX launch sequence with a quantum-transition visual language
- Theme-aware light/dark EntangleX logo assets
- Quantum-inspired professional background treatment
- Smooth modal transitions with reduced-motion support
- Phase 17–18 decision center accessible from the toolbar and sidebar
- Live evidence cockpit for experiments, benchmarks, explainability reports and predictions
- JSON evidence-summary export
- Settings center with Appearance, General, Experiments & ML, Quantum, Benchmarking, Privacy & Security, Notifications, Data & Storage, Explainability, Prediction, Advanced and About sections
- Local browser preference persistence and reset controls
- Mobile-responsive settings and decision-center layouts

## Preservation

The certified Phase 1–16 routes, controls, scientific warnings, light/dark theme persistence and real-data-only result behavior remain intact. The Phase 18 layer is additive and does not create metrics, predictions or evidence when the backend has no records.
